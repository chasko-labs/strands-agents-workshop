# Dynasty Debate Skill

When a user asks you to compare players or debate a dynasty topic, follow this workflow:

## Steps

1. **Identify the subjects** — which players, games, or aspects are being compared?
2. **Look up data for each subject** — use lookup_player and get_season_stats for each
3. **Establish the comparison dimensions** — stats, role on team, clutch moments, narrative
4. **Present findings side by side** — cite specific numbers for each
5. **Connect to the bigger picture** — how did each contribute to the dynasty?
6. **Offer a verdict** — take a position supported by the evidence, name what's debatable

## Example prompts that activate this skill

- "Who was more valuable, Dillon or Branch?"
- "Was the defense or offense more important to the 2004 championship?"
- "Compare the Week 8 loss to the Super Bowl — what changed?"

## Quality standard

- Never declare a winner without citing at least two specific data points per subject
- Acknowledge the counter-argument explicitly
- Name what's unknown or unmeasurable (leadership, locker room presence)
