# Dynasty Context

Background knowledge to inform analysis (not queryable via tools):

## The Trade That Changed Everything

Corey Dillon was acquired from Cincinnati in a trade days before the 2004 draft. He was disgruntled after 7 seasons without a playoff appearance. His arrival transformed the offense from pass-first to balanced, taking pressure off Brady.

## The Cornerback Crisis

The team lost both starting CBs to season-ending injuries: Ty Law (Week 8 foot) and Tyrone Poole (Week 6 knee). They won a Super Bowl using undrafted free agent Randall Gay, second-year Asante Samuel, and Troy Brown playing both ways.

## Troy Brown — The Ultimate Team Player

WR/CB/PR. When the cornerback crisis hit, Brown volunteered to play defense. He wasn't just a warm body — he made plays on both sides of the ball.

## Coaching Exodus

After the Super Bowl, both coordinators left: Charlie Weis to Notre Dame (HC), Romeo Crennel to Cleveland (HC). This makes the 2004 achievement a capstone — the last season of the original dynasty coaching staff intact.

## The 21-Game Win Streak

Carried from October 2003 through October 2004 (spanning both seasons). Ended Week 8 at Pittsburgh 34-20. At the time, an NFL record for consecutive regular season and playoff wins.

## Dynasty Confirmation

The 2004 championship made them the second team to win 3 Super Bowls in 4 years (after the 1992-95 Cowboys). This is when "dynasty" became undeniable.
